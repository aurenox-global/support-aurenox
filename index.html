❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Does Aurenox Global collect my personal data?
No. Our apps (including SuitAI) are designed to work offline by default. We do not collect, store, or share any user data. No telemetry, no analytics, no cloud sync.

💰 Are your apps free?
Yes. All current Aurenox Global software is completely free, with no subscriptions or in‑app purchases.

📱 Which platforms do you support?
We support iOS, iPadOS, macOS, Windows, Linux and Android. Each app specifies its exact requirements.

⚙️ How do I report a security issue?
Please email us directly at security@aurenox.com. We take privacy and security seriously.
